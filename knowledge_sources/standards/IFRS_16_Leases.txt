IFRS 16 — Leases
Source: Wikipedia (CC BY-SA), summary for reference/education.

## Objective and Scope
IFRS 16 provides guidance on accounting for leases. Published January 2016; effective 1 January 2019, replacing IAS 17.

## Motivation
Modernizes lease accounting, ending guesswork in calculating lease obligations and providing transparency on lease assets and liabilities. A key driver was eliminating off-balance-sheet financing.

## Lessee Accounting
Lessees recognize two elements for substantially all leases (bringing them on-balance-sheet):
- Right-of-Use Asset: the lessee's right to use the leased property.
- Lease Liability: the obligation to make lease payments.

## Lessor Accounting
Carries forward IAS 17 requirements: lessors continue to classify leases as operating or finance leases.

## Transition
Permits a modified retrospective approach (comparatives unchanged; cumulative effect as an opening equity adjustment) rather than full retrospective restatement.

## US GAAP Convergence
Developed with the FASB, but IFRS 16 eliminates the lessee operating/finance classification — a distinction retained under US GAAP ASC 842.
