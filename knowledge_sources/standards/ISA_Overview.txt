International Standards on Auditing (ISA) — Overview
Source: Wikipedia (CC BY-SA), summary for reference/education.

## Definition and Issuance
ISAs are professional standards for the auditing of financial information, issued by the International Auditing and Assurance Standards Board (IAASB), which operates under the International Federation of Accountants (IFAC).

## Coverage Areas
- Auditor responsibilities and audit planning
- Internal control evaluation
- Audit evidence collection
- Using the work of experts
- Audit conclusions and reporting
- Specialized audit areas

## Global Application
EU: The Audit Directive (May 2006) mandates ISA application for statutory audits across member states. The European Court of Auditors and the UN Board of Auditors apply/adopt ISAs.

## Structure
IFAC/IAASB maintain the authoritative standards listing. ISAs are numbered (e.g. ISA 200 overall objectives; ISA 315 risk assessment; ISA 320 materiality; ISA 330 responses to risk; ISA 500 audit evidence; ISA 530 audit sampling; ISA 700 forming an opinion and reporting).
