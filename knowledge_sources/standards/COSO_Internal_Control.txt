COSO — Internal Control Integrated Framework & ERM
Source: Wikipedia (CC BY-SA), summary for reference/education.

## Overview
The Committee of Sponsoring Organizations of the Treadway Commission (COSO), founded 1985, published the Internal Control – Integrated Framework in 1992 (updated 2013).

## Five Components of Internal Control
1. Control Environment — organizational culture and tone: integrity, ethical values, management's operating style, delegation of authority, people management.
2. Risk Assessment — identify and analyze risks to objectives; set clear goals; consider fraud potential; monitor changes.
3. Control Activities — policies and procedures (approvals, authorizations, verifications, reconciliations, segregation of duties) that ensure directives are executed and risks addressed.
4. Information and Communication — systems producing operational, financial, and compliance reports; communication throughout the entity, including fraud-reporting procedures.
5. Monitoring — evaluating performance over time via ongoing activities or separate assessments; deficiencies reported and corrected promptly.

## Enterprise Risk Management (ERM, 2004) — eight components
Internal environment, objective setting, event identification, risk assessment, risk response, control activities, information/communication, monitoring — aligned to strategic, operational, reporting, and compliance objectives.

Inherent limitations: human error and management override.
